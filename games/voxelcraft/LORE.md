# The Upturn — a lore bible for Voxelcraft

Everything in this file is original fiction. It exists to art-direct the game:
palettes, silhouettes, sounds, and copy all trace back here.

## The world

Long ago the world **turned over**. Nobody remembers why — the stories disagree
on whether it was a bargain, an accident, or a very large sneeze. What was sky
became stone; what was seabed became sky. That's why the night above the Upturn
glitters like a drained ocean floor (the stars are said to be old pearls), and
why meteor-stones sometimes shake loose and fall back down.

The land remembers the flip. It rose in terraces and stacked hills, forests
knotted themselves back together, and deep below, the old sky still hollows out
caves where sunlight has never been reintroduced.

## The Wanderlings

You are a **Wanderling** — one of the tinkering, satchel-carrying folk who
resettled the Upturn. Wanderlings spent so many generations walking on their
hands (the ground used to be the ceiling, it was safer that way) that their
bodies committed to the arrangement:

- **Their hands are feet.** Arms end in sturdy, flat, big-toed feet. A
  Wanderling doesn't punch a block loose — it *kicks* it loose, with a
  hand-foot. It's considered good manners.
- **Their feet are hands.** Legs end in broad, four-fingered hands (plus
  thumb). Wonderful grip on cliff edges. Terrible in mittens.

Wanderlings dress in rust-orange tunics with mustard trim over charcoal
trousers. Their skin is a pale bog-mint — a leftover, they say, from
generations of standing upside-down in marsh light. They carry everything in a
**Satchel**, and their tidy little field journals stamp counts on everything
(×12, ×48) like museum specimens.

## The creatures

- **Snuffet** — the meadow grazer. Round as a loaf, moss growing on its back,
  ears like drooping banners, a soft trunk for snuffling seeds out of tall
  grass. Harmless; flees with an indignant *snuff* when kicked.
- **Murkling** — what's left of the shadows the flip couldn't shake off. Thin,
  charcoal-blue, almost invisible after dusk. They dislike torchlight and
  anything that owns a satchel. Gone by dawn, as if embarrassed.
- **Stalkid** — a squat lurker of the old-sky caves, mottled like the stone it
  sits against. It doesn't wander. It *waits*. Miners say: if a rock has knees,
  leave it alone.
- **Prickleback** — a desert slab of patience covered in spines. Bothers
  nobody; ruins the day of anyone who bothers it (or leans on it).
- **Mirewalker** — a tall, kelp-shouldered thing that patrols the shorelines,
  dredging the flooded ruins of the pre-flip world. Slow, enormous, and not
  interested in your opinion. The brave hunt them for the old-world salvage
  they carry.

## Tone

Cozy, dry-witted, a little melancholy at night. Danger is real but polite —
it announces itself. Torchlight means safety in a very literal sense: the
things of the old sky won't come near it.
